## 🌟 Project Overview
🚀 This project analyzes the **Northwind database** using SQL to extract business insights across customers, sales, products, suppliers, and operations.

💡 Focus Areas:
- 👥 Customer Behavior  
- 💰 Sales Performance  
- 📦 Product & Inventory  
- 🚚 Supply Chain  
- 📊 Business Growth Trends  

---

## 🎯 Objectives
✔ Identify top customers and revenue contribution  
✔ Analyze sales trends and order behavior  
✔ Evaluate product performance & inventory  
✔ Understand supplier efficiency  
✔ Measure employee productivity  
✔ Detect seasonal sales patterns
